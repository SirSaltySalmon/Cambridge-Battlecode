Why the notes' builds lose (per-champion sums, variant vs v151, seed 0):
  Vesper gets the most gold by far (v151: ~3.6k spent a game, Marcella 2.1k, Prism 1.8k, HoR 0.6k, Ryth 0.5k, Willow
  0.2k), so her build decides most of it. The notes' builds drop her Hunter (+50 gold a kill; she has ~9 kills a game)
  and Skirmish: Vesper deaths 352 / 414 / 452 vs 299 / 314 / 315, kills 1244 / 1259 / 1200 vs 1395 / 1379 / 1451,
  gold earned -89k / -113k / (bcoll2 equal, but the team -16%). Our bot fights champions a lot; farm-only builds suit
  teams that avoid fights. Greed: 20 shards are worth less than 4 Power emblems (24) until the 25th, and her late
  health ratio fell (Greed S + Power H vs Power H/S/S).
Conclusion: keep v151's build. btreas (+1.3% banked, +7% gold over 3 seeds) and vbru (+2.0%, 1 seed) are inside noise;
  btreas is the only candidate for a ladder A/B.
