v170 SUMMARY

v170 is v155 plus a dedicated Prism team on gem-cluster maps. It only changes play on maps where over 70% of
camps are gem clusters, which is 9 of the 70 local maps. Every other map uses v155's strategy.
Folder: bots/v170. Platform version: v170. It was called local v169 during testing.


THE MAIN ISSUE

On those maps, v158 and the earlier Prism versions died early, fell behind on farming, and lost races while
holding unbanked gems. v158 went 6-24 on 30 test games there.


PROBLEMS AND WHAT V170 DOES

1. Wrong roster for these maps
   Evidence: later ladder tests went from 5-10 to 9-6 as the team was rebuilt around Prism.
   Change: drafts Prism, Heart of Ruin, Marcella, Ryth and Willow, with Willow replacing Ryth on 4 picks.

2. Gems weren't building up on Prism, whose Focus grows with her bag
   Evidence: bags were split, and allies who stood on rewards took gems.
   Change: she's first in line within 8 tiles, like a funnelled Vesper, and an ally can take a reward she's
   far from.

3. Prism's deposit took too long or got broken
   Evidence: tributes of 81+ turns rarely finished, 5 of 67 in one version.
   Change: she holds until tributed + bag reaches 1000, but starts earlier if the round limit, the enemy's pace
   or the team's income calls for it, and allies guard her.

4. Team drifted away from her and fought without her
   Evidence: allies were near her only 60% of the time, and 39% of their fight turns were far from her.
   Change: the team stays with her during captures, allies leave fights away from her unless caught, and Willow
   stays within 3 tiles.

5. Fragile Prism died early
   Evidence: first deaths came as early as rounds 46-92, with no items.
   Change: until she has about 1000 max health, she stays behind melee allies.

6. Team cleared the wrong camps
   Evidence: the plan ignored who was closer, and half its groups were on the enemy side.
   Change: groups we reach no later than the enemy come first, and gem clusters are valued highest.

7. Items didn't suit the plan
   Evidence: Monster, Jungle and Treasure were in builds meant for other strategies.
   Change: Prism buys Durability, Prospector, Tribute, then Power Health, and the team buys no Monster, Jungle
   or Treasure.


RESULTS ON THE LADDER (THOSE 9 MAPS ONLY)

Overall:         29-16 over 45 games, with banked gems up 15.1%.
New opponents:   9-6 against 3 teams it had never played.
Against v158:    on the same 30 games, v170 went 20-10 and v158 went 6-24.
Biggest gains:   v168's changes took it from 6-9 to 9-6, and Willow on 4 picks plus Prism staying back then took
                 it from 16-14 to 20-10.


WHAT IT DOESN'T SOLVE

00gem_fields:     0 of 6, likely from early fights around spawns that sit very close together.
Gold:             Prism takes most of the team's gold while allies stay under-equipped, and the first fix tried
                  didn't help.
Top-50 teams:     still the hardest level, at 7-8 across the three rank 45 opponents.
Other 61 maps:    v170 hasn't been compared with v158 there, so it isn't proven as a live replacement.
